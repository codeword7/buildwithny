Hi, I'm Neeraj —
I build things that save time

Engineer by trade. Founder of Pandalabs LLC. I build custom AI workflows that connect the tools accounting firms already run — closing the manual handoffs between QBO, Karbon, and the rest of the stack.

I've spent years as a software engineer building systems that make complex things simple. Somewhere along the way I realised the most valuable thing I could build wasn't another app — it was time back for people drowning in repetitive work.

That led me to accounting firms. When I looked closely at how small and mid-sized firms operate, I saw the same story everywhere: talented people spending 10 to 15 hours a week coding transactions, chasing client documents, and grinding through month-end close — work that modern AI can handle in a fraction of the time.

"The goal was never to replace anyone. It was to take the repetitive work off their plate so the team can focus on what actually needs human judgment."

What I Build

Through BuildWithNY, I build custom AI-powered workflows for accounting and bookkeeping firms. Not generic tools — systems built specifically around your firm's processes, your chart of accounts, your clients.

Everything I build keeps a human in control. The AI does the heavy lifting; your team approves. Every action is logged with a full audit trail. Built in two weeks, fixed fee, and you own it permanently — no subscriptions, no lock-in.
